Why we exist

Good businesses should be easier to find.

You know the details of your work because you do it every day. A potential customer may be learning about it for the first time. Your website needs to help them understand what you offer and whether it’s right for them.

We help you explain that work clearly and make it easier to find through search. That means spending time on your business, your customers and the questions that come up before someone books.

What you can expect.

Start with the business.

We ask about your services, the jobs you want and how much new work you can take on before recommending a plan.

Know what we’re working on.

We agree on the work, who is responsible and how we’ll report progress. You should know what is happening and why.

Check the details.

We check credentials, service areas and business information with you before they go on the site.

Talk about the enquiries.

More traffic only tells part of the story. We also want to know whether enquiries are relevant and what customers are asking for.

A few common questions.

Where is Wayfound based?

We’re based in Egypt and work remotely with businesses in the US and Canada.

What should we prepare for the audit call?

Have your website handy and think about the services you’d like more enquiries for. It also helps to know your service areas and what marketing you’ve tried. You won’t need to share passwords.
